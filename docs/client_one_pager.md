# What this service does — in plain English

**The problem.** Every month, supplier invoices arrive as PDFs — each
vendor uses a different layout — and sales come out of two different
systems in messy spreadsheet exports. Someone has to retype and fix it all
by hand before the books can be done.

**What you send.** One folder: the month's invoice PDFs, the two sales
exports, and your clean customer and vendor lists.

**What you get back.** One Excel workbook you can trust:

- every invoice captured, with its line items;
- both sales systems in one tidy table;
- a list of customers matched to your master list;
- a **Review** sheet of the handful of things that need a human eye; and
- an **Issues** sheet that records every single fix, drop or flag — file,
  row, old value, new value, and why.

You also get a one-page report (web page and PDF) summarising what came
in, what came out, and what needs attention.

**What gets checked.** Duplicate invoices, totals that don't add up,
vendor tax IDs that don't match your records, invoices dated in the
future, scanned PDFs that need manual entry, duplicate sales rows,
unknown or misspelled customer names, refunds with no matching sale,
negative quantities, and unreadable amounts.

**What it will not do.** It does not read scanned images (no OCR — it
lists them for you instead). A brand-new invoice layout needs a short
configuration block added once per vendor.

*This page describes a portfolio demonstration built on simulated data
("Harbor & Pine Supply" is fictional). It describes what the tool checks
and produces; it makes no claims about savings or accuracy on real data.*
